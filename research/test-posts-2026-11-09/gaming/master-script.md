# Best nostalgic games for handhelds — master script

Voiceover: local Kokoro-82M `af_bella`; no on-screen avatar.

Target master runtime: approximately 75–85 seconds at natural speed.

## Exact spoken script (English)

Nostalgia does not have to mean nineteen ninety-two. These five modern classics feel like pulling the perfect cartridge from a drawer — and every one belongs on a handheld.

At number five, Transistor. Its neon city, deliberate combat and dreamy atmosphere make every short session feel like a lost late-night memory.

Number four is Celeste. The controls are immediate, the rooms are compact, and every hard-earned checkpoint makes “one more try” impossible to resist.

At number three, Hollow Knight turns a small screen into a huge, mysterious world. Headphones on, lights down, disappear for an hour.

Number two is Stardew Valley: the ultimate comfort game. Water a few crops for ten minutes — or look up and discover the entire evening is gone.

And number one is Hades. Fast runs, sharp controls and a story that keeps unfolding make it the rare action game that works for both a quick break and a long trip.

The real test is not the release year. It is whether fifteen spare minutes can turn into a memory. Which one lives on your handheld?
