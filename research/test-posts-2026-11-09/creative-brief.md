# Premium test-post production brief

Date: 2026-11-09

## Deliverable family

Each of the two editorial posts is built from one approved master script, licensed source footage and a locally generated Kokoro voiceover. No avatar, talking head, lip sync or HeyGen master is required. The edit family contains:

- TikTok 9:16: approximately 40–50 seconds, faster commentary and ZOOM gameplay/product treatment.
- Instagram Reels 9:16: approximately 55–65 seconds, SAFE framing where labels or UI would otherwise be cropped, with a single save/share CTA.
- YouTube 16:9: the complete master argument, approximately 75–90 seconds, with a dedicated landscape thumbnail.
- ZOOM reference variant 9:16: wide → zoom 1 → wide → zoom 2, with exact phase durations.
- SAFE reference variant 9:16: full source width over a subdued blurred fill whenever cropping would remove factual detail.

No version is published automatically. All versions remain local and awaiting owner review.

## Shared production rules

- Kokoro voiceover uses the reviewed `af_bella` preset; every segment receives fresh text-hash, duration and SHA-256 provenance.
- Full burned-in English captions plus a plain UTF-8 SRT sidecar.
- One dominant focal point per beat; captions, graphics and B-roll never compete at equal strength.
- Mostly motivated hard cuts and match cuts; stylized wipes are limited to real chapter or comparison changes.
- Original code-composed music and restrained procedural SFX.
- Real producer footage/spec pages are evidence; generated AI imagery is illustrative and labelled as such in the asset ledger.
- Every factual hardware claim is attached to an official source and a qualification.
- One integrated preview review and at most one consolidated correction batch per finished master.

## Art direction

### Gaming — “Neon cartridge memory”

Warm CRT bloom, cobalt shadows, amber/orange highlights, restrained scan-line texture, crisp cel-shaded type, cartridge-like chapter stamps and pixel-grid match cuts. Gameplay remains factual and unfiltered enough to judge. The style evokes memory without pretending the modern games are from the 1990s.

### Local AI — “Precision lab editorial”

Swiss precision with obsidian, warm white and electric cyan; large numeric typography, exact memory-capacity blocks, clean bandwidth rails, source-page detail reveals and smooth technical linework. No generic dashboard wall and no decorative fake data.
