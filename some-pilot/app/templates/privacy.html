Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Data we process

The service processes connected social account identifiers, OAuth credentials, videos, captions, content evidence, technical checks, publication records and performance metrics needed to operate the owner-controlled publishing workflow.

How data is used

Data is used to create and review videos, publish owner-approved content, collect performance statistics, maintain audit records and improve content decisions. No personal data is sold.

Service providers

Content and credentials are processed using Contabo hosting and the connected platforms: Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram/Facebook and TikTok when configured. Claude API processes research text, scripts and claim reviews; social credentials are not sent to Claude.

Storage and retention

Credentials are stored in a root-protected server configuration. Backups are encrypted. Data is retained only as needed for operation, audit, backup and learning records. Backup retention is managed by the service owner.

Your choices

You can disconnect apps through the relevant platform and request deletion or access through Instagram @pixelandparameters. Revoking access does not automatically delete content already published on a platform.

Security and contact

Reasonable technical controls include restricted administration, encrypted HTTPS transport, owner authentication, signed media links and approval hashes. No system can guarantee absolute security. Questions and data requests can be sent through Instagram @pixelandparameters.
